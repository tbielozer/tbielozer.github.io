About

I have spent the past 3 years working on a Computer Engineering degree at Brigham Young University. I am passionate about embedded design, robotics, and sensor and actuator interfacing.

Currenlty I work at BYU's Electronic Prototyping Information Center (EPICenter) where I am the team lead for PCB production, work on demos for the department, give trainings, and offer consultation for research and personal products at BYU.

Embedded System · Wireless Communication

Old Video Game Remote

I had build a simple emulator for old Nintendo and Atari games on a pi 4 and wanted a remote to play the games. Using an ESP32 I was able to create a remote with a joystick for the dpad and buttons for the other controls, similar to a Super Nintendo controller. I created the code library myself and debugged it and the circuit on a breadboard. I later designed and milled a PCB for the remote.
